## Arithmetic and answer checks
- `.venv/Scripts/python -m pytest tests/calc -q -k hyperfine-21cm`: 22 passed.
- Hyperfine constant, recomputed by hand: $H_{\text{contact}} = \frac{8\pi}{3}\frac{g_pe^2}{2m_em_pc^2}\vec S_e\cdot\vec S_p\,\delta^3(\vec r)$; times $1/\pi a_0^3$ with $a_0^3 = \hbar^6/m_e^3e^6$ gives $\Delta E_{\text{HF}} = \frac{4}{3}g_p\frac{m_e^2e^8}{m_pc^2\hbar^4} = \frac{4}{3}g_p\frac{m_e}{m_p}\alpha^4m_ec^2$. Correct (this is the Fermi result without reduced-mass and electron $g - 2$ corrections).
- Angular average: $\langle\hat r_i\hat r_j\rangle = \frac{1}{3}\delta_{ij}$ (trace 1), so $3\hat r_i\hat r_j - \delta_{ij}$ averages to 0. Correct.

- Lowering: $J_-|1,1\rangle = \hbar\sqrt{2}|1,0\rangle$; $(J_-^{(1)} + J_-^{(2)})|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle = \hbar(|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle + |\uparrow\downarrow\rangle)$. Correct.
