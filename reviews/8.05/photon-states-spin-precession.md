Physics checks done independently:
- Maxwell check: with $E_x = A\omega q\sin kz$, $cB_y = Ap\cos kz$, $A = \sqrt{2/\epsilon_0V}$, Ampère ($\partial_tE_x = -c^2\partial_zB_y$) gives $\dot q = p$ and Faraday ($\partial_tB_y = -\partial_zE_x$) gives $\dot p = -\omega^2q$: exactly Hamilton's (and Heisenberg's) equations of $\frac{1}{2}(p^2 + \omega^2q^2)$. The page's claim is right.
- Precession sign: $H_S\chi_{z\pm} = \mp\gamma B\frac{\hbar}{2}\chi_{z\pm}$, so the phases are $e^{\pm i\gamma Bt/2}$; factoring $e^{i\gamma Bt/2}$ leaves $e^{i(\phi_0 - \gamma Bt)}$ on $\chi_{z-}$: $\phi(t) = \phi_0 - \gamma Bt$, a rotation by $-\gamma Bt$ about $z$, consistent with $\vec\omega_L = -\gamma\vec B$. Correct.
- Classical: $d\vec S/dt = \vec\mu\times\vec B = \gamma\vec S\times\vec B = -\gamma\vec B\times\vec S$, of the form $\vec\omega\times\vec S$ with $\vec\omega = -\gamma\vec B$. Correct.
- General Hamiltonian: $\sigma_2$ has $-i$ in the upper right, so $g_1\sigma_1 + g_2\sigma_2$ gives $g_1 - ig_2$ upper right and $g_1 + ig_2$ lower left. Correct. $\vec g\cdot\vec\sigma = \frac{2}{\hbar}\vec g\cdot\vec S$ gives $\vec\omega_L = 2\vec g/\hbar$. Correct.
