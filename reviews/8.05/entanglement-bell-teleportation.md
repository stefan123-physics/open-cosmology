## Arithmetic and answer checks
Recomputed with NumPy/SciPy (script in the reviewer's scratch folder, not the repo):
- Bell states: $(1\otimes\sigma_i)\Phi_0$ gives exactly the page's $\Phi_1$, $\Phi_2 = \frac{i}{\sqrt2}(|+-\rangle - |-+\rangle)$, $\Phi_3$. Gram matrix of $\Phi_0..\Phi_3$ is the identity. Coefficient-matrix determinants $\pm\frac12$, all non-zero.
- Inversion formulas (3.58): all four verified, including the signs $\Phi_1 \mp i\Phi_2$.
- Teleportation identity: $\Phi_0^{AB}\otimes\psi_C$, reordered A, C, B, equals $\frac12\sum_\mu \Phi_\mu^{AC}\otimes\sigma_\mu\psi_B$ for a random complex $\psi$. The page's expanded four-term state is also correct. Bob's corrections ($\sigma_\mu$, since $\sigma_\mu^2 = 1$) are right.
- $\exp\left(\frac{i\pi}{2}(-1 + \sigma_1)\right) = \sigma_1$: verified with `expm`; each intermediate step on the page is correct.
- Worked example (a): determinant $\frac14 - \frac14 = 0$; factorisation $\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(|+\rangle + |-\rangle)\otimes\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(|+\rangle + |-\rangle)$, both spins along $+x$. Correct.
