# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.962.json

Scope and method: all 104 records of `data/glossary/8.962.json` (MIT 8.962 General Relativity, Hughes, Year V) were read against docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §2 and §8 and the scoping rules in `lib/glossary.ts` (pages beat courses beat the general meaning). That is 102 entries plus the two meanings-only records, `event-horizon` (entry in 8.286.json) and `weight` (entry in 8.01.json). Every lesson in every course was scanned with the site's own loader and matcher (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor`), with all glossary files loaded so that longest match across files applies. For each 8.962 entry, the first matched use on each page was listed with the meaning that would apply there, and every use outside 8.962 was read in context. Facts, numbers, dates and conventions were checked against the 8.962 lessons: signature (−, +, +, +), c = 1, Carroll's conventions, weight −2 for the metric determinant and weight 1 for the Levi-Civita symbol, and ρ_curv = −3κ/(8πG a²). The plain-English rules were also checked (length ≤ 25 words, genus and difference, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling), along with `say`, `kind`, `origin`, `everyday` and forms. The loader reports no schema errors for this file. Every `related` id and `symbols` id exists, no form is claimed by two entries, no short meaning is over 25 words, and there are no em dashes.

Verdict: the general-relativity meanings are accurate and agree with the 8.962 lessons. Facts checked and found right include photon orbit at 1.5 horizon radii, ISCO at 3 horizon radii, Buchdahl 9/4, Kretschmann 48G²M²/r⁶, 20 = 10 + 10 Riemann components, n(n + 1)/2 Killing vectors, the Ricci scalar 2/a² of a sphere, Kerr 1963, Isaacson 1968, the 370 km/s dipole, and the distance relations. The problems come from general meanings reaching pages in other courses through common forms: "tensor" in quantum "tensor product", "raising and lowering" operators, the CMB "quadrupole", "symmetrise" for quantum states, Birkhoff's theorem in 8.902, "Newtonian limit" in MOND, and "test mass" for LIGO mirrors. There are also a few imprecise longs. **Not ready to merge until the 3 errors are fixed; the warnings should be fixed too.**

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- tensor: new entry `tensor-product` (general quantum/linear-algebra meaning), so "tensor product" no longer gets the index meaning.
- raising-and-lowering-indices: bare form "raising and lowering" dropped; long rewritten in words.
- quadrupole-moment: bare form "quadrupole" dropped; new general entry `quadrupole` (a pattern or distribution stretched along an axis, covering the CMB quadrupole and electric quadrupoles).
- isaacson-tensor: now owns "Isaacson stress-energy tensor(s)" (moved from stress-energy-tensor).
- four-velocity: "squared length" with the signature stated.
- dummy-index, ricci-tensor, perfect-fluid, killings-equation, lorenz-gauge: index formulas replaced by words; dummy-index short says "usually once up and once down".
- einstein-hilbert-action: history reworded as suggested.
- kerr-metric: bare form "Kerr" dropped.
- symmetrisation, antisymmetrisation: shorts broadened to cover identical particles. An entry for the symmetrization postulate is left to the 8.06 owner.
- equation-of-state: short broadened to any state variables.
- newtonian-limit: page meaning for `mond-tests`.
- birkhoffs-theorem: 8.902 meaning for the inside form.
- test-body: page meanings for `magnetic-monopoles` and `div-curl-and-maxwell`.
- Info: polytrope short without caret; Buchdahl short names the radius; EHT dating relative to Guth's 2013 lectures; Schwarzschild 1915/1916; Levi-Civita respelling; British spellings in longs; Klein–Gordon short convention-free; horizon written 2GM/c² throughout; weight long added; everyday notes for contraction, connection, chirp; radiation-dominated short covers eras. Not done: wider scopes for contraction/connection in 8.07/8.286 and entries for halo or initial mass function (left to those courses' writers).
