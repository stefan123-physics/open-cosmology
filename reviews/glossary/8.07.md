- **tensor:** added a meaning scoped to the ten pages that write "tensor product" (the joining of two quantum state spaces). A separate "tensor product" entry is left to the 8.05 writer, who owns that word.
- **lorenz-gauge:** added a meaning for 8.962 (linearized gravity: the condition on the trace-reversed perturbation; Hughes calls it Lorentz gauge).
- **arrow-of-time:** the long now says low initial entropy is the most often heard explanation, which Guth finds incomplete, preferring the idea that the entropy has no upper limit.
- **dalembertian:** the short no longer fixes a sign; the long says books differ (8.07 versus 8.033) and that it is written □² or □.
- **quadrupole:** the short covers the ℓ = 2 pattern over a sphere; 1/r³ moved to the long.
- **multipole-expansion:** added a meaning scoped to inflation-solutions (splitting the CMB sky into patterns numbered by ℓ).
- **damping:** added a meaning for "damped Lyman-α system" (lyman-alpha-forest, big-bang-nucleosynthesis); the general long is now generic.
- **auxiliary-field:** the short now says its curl inside matter is set by the free currents alone.
- **advanced-potentials:** the short was rewritten as suggested.
- **One-sentence longs:** a second sentence was added to octupole, advanced-time, cyclic-permutation and the second meaning of uniqueness-theorem.
- **Info items:** traceless-symmetric-part ("numbers times"), larmor-formula (γ⁶ only for straight-line motion), anticommutative (Dirac's 4 × 4 matrices), ideal-dipole (dipole flow in fluids) and the Wiechert respelling (VEE-khert) are done.
