- Plain-English rules (short ≤ 25 words, genus then difference, not circular, no harder word, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling as the site uses it, i.e. -ize with -ise forms), `long` length (2 to 4 sentences), `say`, `kind`, `everyday`, `origin`. No short exceeds 25 words; no TeX or em dash.

Verdict: the physics is accurate and the 8.044 meanings agree with the lessons. Fix before merging: the problems are mostly **forms that match other senses** (particle reservoirs in 8.08 shown as heat baths, the everyday "indistinguishable", "thermal noise" of a cantilever, the 18.03 equilibrium meaning on spring pages), one wrong statement in a short meaning (central limit theorem), the enthalpy short, and four one-sentence `long` texts.
