# Glossary review: 18.03 (data/glossary/18.03.json)

Independent review, 2026-11-19. All 88 records were checked (87 entries plus the extra 18.03 meaning for `homogeneous`, whose entry is in `data/glossary/academic.json`).

What was checked:

- Each meaning against the lessons that use the word. Every page that would mark each entry was found with the loader's own rules: the prose of the page (as `proseOf`), all forms, longest match first, whole words, first use per page, and the meaning chosen by page, then course, then general. That gave 1,400 marked uses in all courses. Wrong senses in other courses were looked for especially hard.
- Facts and formulas against the 18.03 lessons: damping cases, practical resonance, Volterra's principle, the weight and transfer functions, Euler, Heun and Runge–Kutta, the Wronskian, the logistic equation, Bernoulli and homogeneous substitutions, and the eigenvalue cases.
- Scoped pages: every page in a `pages` list uses the word in that sense, and every `introducedIn` slug exists. No problems were found here.
- The plain-English rules: at most 25 words, genus then difference, not circular, no harder word than the headword, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling. Also `say`, `kind`, `everyday` and `origin`, and every `related` and `symbols` id. All ids resolve, there is no em dash or TeX, and every short meaning is within 25 words.

**Verdict:** the 18.03 meanings themselves are accurate and well written. The errors are mostly broad words (stable, unstable, amplitude, periodic, trace, homogeneous) and loose forms that mark the wrong sense on pages of other courses, plus one meaning (limit cycle) that disagrees with its 18.03 lesson. Fix the 13 errors before merging.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All 13 errors and 9 warnings fixed; most info items taken.

- **stable**: particle meaning widened ("particle, nucleus, atomic state or vacuum") and given the three extra pages; kind changed to maths (also for unstable).
- **unstable**: new decay meaning for the five pages listed.
- **amplitude**: new quantum meaning (complex number whose squared size gives a probability) scoped to the 14 quantum pages that use that sense; pages where the word means the size of an oscillation keep the general meaning.
- **periodic**: new entry `periodic-table`, so the table is matched by the longer form.
- **trace**: the course scopes are replaced by one explicit list of 34 pages that use the matrix or tensor sense, leaving out `limit-cycles` and `potentials-gauges-retarded-solutions`; added an `everyday` note on the verb. (The duplicated meaning is gone.)
- **homogeneous**: page meaning for `substitution-methods` (y′ = F(y/x)); the linear-equation meaning copied onto the eight pages of other courses; a "homogeneous of degree n" meaning for the two 8.09 pages.
- **normalized-solutions**: scoped to 18.03; headword now British ("normalised solutions"), -ized spellings as forms.
- **unit-step-function**: forms "unit steps" and "unit step functions" dropped.
- **polynomial-operator**: generalised to a polynomial in any operator.
- **first-order-system**: scoped to 18.03 (plus `differential-equations-exp-at`); Strang's difference-equation sense added for `diagonalization-and-powers`.
- **constant-coefficients**: series-coefficient meaning for `separation-of-variables-spherical`.
- **limit-cycle**: 18.03-scoped meaning (attracting from both sides only); the general meaning, which allows repelling cycles, stays for 8.09.
- Warnings: critically damped ("without oscillating", may cross once), orthogonal (matrix sentence in `long`), characteristic equation (covers matrices), Jacobian matrix (general), analytic (analytical-mechanics meaning for `rigid-body-coordinates-euler-angles`), integrating factor (general), singularity (8.08 meaning; "where a solution blows up"), weight function (no "convolving" in the short), particular solution (Ax = b in `long`).
- Info items taken: eigenvalue (negative and complex), Bernoulli and logistic in the lesson's form, argument (form "arguments", page `sinusoidal-input-response`), asymptotically stable (also of an equation), pseudo-frequency respelling, Runge–Kutta ("one RK2 method"), first-order linear equation ("never multiplied together").
