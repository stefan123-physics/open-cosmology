Physics and maths in the definitions are almost all correct and clearly written. The problems are nearly all about **where a meaning lands**: general or course-wide meanings that reach pages with another sense, entry-level `everyday` notes shown next to the wrong meaning, and missing longer entries ("moment of inertia", "order of magnitude") so that the shorter word's meaning appears inside a phrase.

Note on the fix mechanics: `termMeaningFor` ignores `courses` on a meaning that also has `pages` (`!m.pages && m.courses…`). So to add single pages to a meaning that is already course-scoped, add a **separate copy** of that meaning with `pages`, not a `pages` list on the course-scoped one.

## What is good

- Physics and maths content is accurate throughout: every number listed above checks out, signs and formulas (T² = 4π²r³/(GM), ρc = 3H²/(8πG), 6πηrv, dP/dr = −ρg, v = Rω, x = R(θ − sin θ)) are right.
- Short meanings follow genus-then-difference, are plain and non-circular, under 25 words, British spelling, no TeX, no em dash. `say` respellings are sensible (vuh-LOSS-ih-tee, sen-TRIP-ih-tul, koh-ih-FISH-unt uv res-tih-TYOO-shun, sy-DEER-ee-ul); `origin` lines are right apart from the viscosity nuance. `everyday` notes (acceleration, velocity, work, power, normal force, conserved, conservative, tension, weight) are accurate and useful; the problem is only that two of them show with a second, unrelated sense.
- The scoping already in place works well in most cases: work and power are page-scoped and never mark the everyday "work"; elastic, impulse, turning-point, scalar (8.962), separation-of-variables (PDE courses) and the 8.286 cyclic universe all land on the right pages.

## Verdict

Not ready to merge as is. The definitions themselves are sound; the 16 errors are mis-marks on real pages (a wrong sense shown to the reader) plus one wrong `introducedIn`. Nearly all are fixed by adding page-scoped copies of existing meanings and three new entries ("moment of inertia", "order of magnitude", optionally "comoving"), plus `caseSensitive` on hertz. After the fixes, rerun the first-use listing for spin, weight, tension, tangent, cyclic, magnitude, inertia and critical-density to confirm each page gets the intended meaning.
