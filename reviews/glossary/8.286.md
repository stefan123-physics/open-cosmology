## Verdict

The pilot file is in good shape: the writing is plain and well structured (genus then difference, no circular definitions, British spelling, no TeX or em dashes, every `related` and `symbols` id valid), the scoping of ambiguous words (matter, radiation, horizon, flat, dust, hyperbolic, left-handed, tunnel) is thoughtful, and the history and attributions (COBE, WMAP, High-Z, SCP, IAU 2018 Hubble–Lemaître, Yang–Mills, Georgi–Glashow, Kruskal) are correct, with post-2013 developments (Hubble tension, DESI hints, inflaton and Higgs inflation) marked as later. It should not be merged as the model for other writers until the five errors are fixed: the W and Z mass ratio, the supersymmetry short meaning, the wrong sense of "flavour" on the Ferreras page, the missing c and √k in the cycloid formulas, and the "metastable" panel contradicting the false-vacuum text. The recombination/decoupling merge, the 15 vs 18 decimal places, and the missing forms "Hubble rate" and "monopoles" are the most useful of the warnings, because they touch the central words on many pages.

Counts: 5 errors, 16 warnings, 6 info.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed in `data/glossary/8.286.json`, with these exceptions noted:

- **Errors:** w-and-z-bosons masses corrected (80 and 91 GeV, about 86 and 97 proton masses); supersymmetry short rewritten (spin one half partners / whole-number-spin partners); flavour gets a page-scoped meaning for Ferreras `dark-matter-particles` (cold, warm, hot dark matter); parametric-solution now gives ct = α(θ − sin θ) and a/√k = α(1 − cos θ); metastable gets a page-scoped meaning for `false-vacuum` (the loose lesson wording is reported to the lead for the page author).
