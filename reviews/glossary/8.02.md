# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.02.json

Reviewer: independent agent, 2026-11-19. Data file not edited.

**What was checked.** All 129 entries were run through the site's own matcher (`lib/glossary.ts`: `loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `termMeaningFor`, `proseOf`, `defaultIntroductions`) over every lesson, listing for each entry the pages where it would be marked, the meaning chosen there and the sentence around the first use. On that basis about 75 entries were read in detail against their 8.02 lessons (`content/year-1/8.02/`) and against pages of other courses where the meaning would show, including every entry with more than one meaning or a scoped meaning (scalar-field, pressure, polarization, in-phase, node, coherent, flux, resistance, in-parallel, generator, resonance) and the field-family entries. Checked: physics and agreement with the lessons, the writing standard of docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8 (≤ 25 words, genus then difference, not circular, plain words, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling), respellings, kinds, risky forms and `introducedIn`. The loader reports no errors; all `related` ids exist; every `short` is within 25 words; no em dashes or TeX.

**Verdict.** Good overall: the 8.02-only words (circuits, magnetism, induction, AC) are accurate, plain and consistent with the lessons, including magnetic susceptibility (M = χₘB₀/μ₀, matches capacitors/transformers pages). The problems are concentrated in (a) two shorts that are physically wrong or contradict the lesson (wave equation, ohmmeter) and (b) general or course-scoped meanings that land on the wrong sense in other courses (node in 8.09, coherent in 8.05, Gauss's law in 18.02, polarization in 8.044). 6 errors, 12 warnings, 11 info.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed; info items fixed except where noted.

- **wave-equation:** short now says the curvature sets how strongly each point accelerates.
- **ohmmeter:** applies a voltage and measures the current, as in `rc-circuits`; long changed to match.
- **node:** the phase-portrait meaning is now scoped to 18.03 and to the two 8.09 pages that use it (`fixed-points-two-dimensions`, `limit-cycles-hopf-bifurcations`), so the Euler-angle and Kepler pages no longer get it. 8.09 owns "line of nodes" and "longitude of the ascending node" as candidates; their entries will take those phrases. Added a meaning for the cosmic web on Ferreras `dark-matter-halos`.
- **coherent:** 8.04 and 8.05 removed from the scope. "coherent states" is an 8.05 candidate, so its writer will add the entry.
- **gauss-law:** short now covers both electric fields and gravity, with genus first ("The law that…").
- **polarization:** `microcanonical-ensemble` added to the dielectric meaning.
- **field:** removed the push/pull clause from the short; it is in the long now. 18.03's "each field has its own tricks" stays as accepted noise.
- **field-theory:** new entry `quantum-field-theory` (forms "quantum field theories", "QFT"), so the longer form wins.
- **flux:** general short now says "a flow of matter or energy (per second, for a flow)"; 8.286 `supernova-evidence` has its own page-scoped meaning for the flux of light.
- **energy:** short says "a closed system keeps its total"; the long notes that a redshifted photon loses energy in an expanding universe.
- **pressure:** introducedIn changed to 8.07 `conductor-surface-forces-capacitance`, which defines pressure as force per area. (No 8.01 lesson explains it.)
- **Laws and experiments:** Young's, Babinet's, Gauss's, Ampère's, Faraday's and Lenz's now start with their genus.
- **lorentz-force:** short is in words; the formula moved to the long.
- **coherent, simple-harmonic-motion, dielectric:** reworded as suggested.
- **circulation:** introducedIn changed to 18.02 `stokes-theorem`.
- **Info items:** resistance now has meanings scoped to the four 18.03 and 8.08 circuit pages. Scalar field now covers the two 8.08 pages. Far-field short has the "if that is not zero" caveat. Gauss is "about twice the Earth's field". Respellings changed to KWON-tyzd, AM-pairz law and TRANZ-vurss. Intensity now covers particle beams. Henry and mutual inductance got long meanings.
- **Left as they are:** AC (only frontmatter matches today), DC on 18.06, charge density wave (an 8.08 phrase), single-word respellings of multi-word terms, and the other entries without a long meaning.
