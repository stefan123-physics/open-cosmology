# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.033.json

Scope and method: all 59 entries of `data/glossary/8.033.json` (MIT 8.033, Year II) were read against docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8 and the scoping rules in `lib/glossary.ts` (pages beat courses beat the general meaning). With the site's own loader and matcher (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor`), every lesson in every course was scanned, and for each entry the first matched use on each page was listed with the meaning that would apply there. Every multi-meaning entry and every general meaning shown outside 8.033 was checked in context. Facts, numbers and dates were checked, along with the plain-English rules (length, genus and difference, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling), `say`, `kind`, `origin`, `everyday` and forms. The loader reports no schema errors, and every `symbols` id exists in `data/symbols.json`.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed in `data/glossary/8.033.json` except where noted; most info items applied.

- proper-distance: added page meanings for `spacetime-interval` (special-relativity sense) and for `schwarzschild-birkhoff-tov`, `schwarzschild-orbits-photon-ring` (distance along space with the metric).
- fine-tuning: new general meaning (a quantity that must be set very precisely for no known reason); the anthropic sense is now scoped to `particle-processes-unsolved-problems`.
- aberration: "up to about 20 arcseconds either side of its mean position".
- event: page meaning for `homogeneous-expansion`. The "event horizon" matches are not fixed here: "event horizon" is owned by the 8.286 glossary file and "Event Horizon Telescope" by the 8.962 file; once those entries exist, longest match takes the phrase (flagged to the lead).
- interval: `time-dilation-length-contraction` removed from the spacetime meaning's pages.
- invariant: short widened as suggested. "adiabatic invariant" is owned by the 8.06 file; its entry will take the phrase by longest match (flagged to the lead).
- galilean-invariance: form "Galilean invariant" dropped.
- annihilation: operator meaning reworded ("turning a state or vector into zero …"); page meaning "Complete destruction." for `speed-of-light-michelson-morley`; origin reworded.
- friedmann-equation: new entry `second-friedmann-equation`.
- strong-equivalence-principle: page meaning for `equivalence-principle-local-lorentz-frames`.
- gauge-freedom: 8.962 course meaning (coordinate freedom).
- timelike, spacelike: shorts now cover separations, directions, paths and surfaces.
- Info: boost long, lorentz-force-law long, lorentz-factor short without symbols (γ moved to the long), proton–proton chain kind "physics", fusion origin, work–kinetic energy theorem forms, 13.8 billion years, longer longs for hulse-taylor-pulsar, muon and proper-length, form "interferometry" dropped. Not changed: the kind of eponymous terms (left to the lead for a site-wide rule) and the weak equivalence principle wording (matches the 8.033 slide).
