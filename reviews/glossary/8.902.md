## Verdict

A strong file. The astrophysics is accurate almost throughout (numbers such as 121.6 nm, 656 nm, −2.35, six baryons per ten billion photons, 50,000 years for equality agree with the lessons), the short meanings are plain and genus-first, and the scoping is mostly careful (colour, virial, relaxed, bias, jet, freeze-out). Three course-scoped or general meanings mark a wrong sense on real pages (growth factor on 8.286 inflation-overview, concentration and hierarchical on Ferreras pages): these are the errors to fix before merging. The warnings are mostly meanings slightly narrower than their uses (core, abundance, anisotropy, sound speed, galaxy, far-infrared), two latent wrong-sense forms (narrow-line region, radio lobes), the garbled Birkhoff short, and about thirty one-sentence long meanings.

Counts: 4 errors, 15 warnings, 9 info.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All four errors and all warnings fixed; most info items taken.

- growth-factor: added a meaning scoped to 8.286 `inflation-overview` (growth by e to the N during inflation).
- concentration: scoped to `potential-density-pairs` and `galaxies-in-halos`; form "concentrations" dropped.
- hierarchical: added a meaning for `simulating-galaxy-formation` (nested cells of a tree code).
- birkhoffs-theorem: short rewritten as proposed.
- core, abundance, sound-speed, far-infrared, galaxy (from thousands of stars), sachs-wolfe-effect (both halves), nfw-profile (one over radius cubed), accelerated-expansion (second derivative moved to the long): shorts rewritten.
- anisotropy: short made general; meaning for the velocity anisotropy β added on `core-cusp-problem`, `galaxy-clusters`, `gravitational-lensing`.
- broad-line-region: narrow-line forms removed, narrow-line region described in the long. radio-galaxy: radio lobe forms removed.
- Respellings: Einstein–de Sitter "EYEN-styne duh SIT-er", BL Lac "bee-el LAK", NFW "en-ef-DUB-ul-yoo", Sérsic "SER-sik".
- gauss-theorem second meaning reworded ("which the lesson credits to Gauss"). Note for the 8.286 owner: the `sphere-metrics` sentence attributes local flatness to Gauss.
- All 29 one-sentence longs now have a second sentence.
- Info: self-interacting dark matter reworded and bare form "self-interacting" dropped; ISCO, Eddington accretion rate, SED and radiation era reworded; -ize spelling made consistent inside photoionization, reionization and ultraviolet-background. Jet, abundance and colour scopes left as they are.
