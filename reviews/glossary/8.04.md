# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.04.json (MIT 8.04 Quantum Physics I)

Independent review, 2026-11-19. All 78 records were checked: 77 entries plus one extra record (`complete`, whose entry is in `data/glossary/18.03.json`). Only one `{ "id", "meanings" }` record exists in the file, not two; `degeneracy` is a full entry here, with two of its three meanings scoped to 8.902, Ferreras and 8.044 pages.

What was checked:

- Every form of every entry was matched against the prose of every page in `content/` with the loader's own code (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor` from `lib/glossary.ts`): whole words, case-insensitive, longest form first, first use per page, scope pages > courses > general. For each marked page the sentence around the first use was compared with the meaning shown there. The file loads without schema errors; every `related` and `symbols` id resolves; every slug in a `pages` list exists and uses the word in the scoped sense.
- Facts against the 8.04 lessons: Bohr radius 0.529 Å = 0.053 nm (`hydrogen-atom`), most probable distance a₀ (`hydrogen-spectrum`), α = e²/(ħc) ≈ 1/137 in Gaussian units, Compton wavelength h/(mc) = 2.426 pm and classical electron radius e²/(mc²) ≈ 2.8 fm (`photoelectric-compton-de-broglie`), h ≈ 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, −13.6 eV/n², n = N + l + 1, Levinson N_b = (δ(0) − δ(∞))/π, A_s = e^(iδ) sin δ, |A_s|² = sin²δ, Breit–Wigner (Γ/2)²/((E − E_α)² + (Γ/2)²) with τ = ħ/Γ, Pauli commutators, Legendre's equation and P₂, Hermite polynomials, Parseval, Malus. All numbers and formulas in the file are correct.
- Plain-English rules (≤ 25 words, genus then difference, not circular, no harder word, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling), `long` length (2 to 4 sentences), `say`, `kind`, `everyday`, `origin`. No TeX, em dash or American spelling found (the -ize forms follow the lessons, with -ise variants as forms).

Verdict: the physics in the file is accurate and the 8.04 meanings agree with the lessons. The problems are almost all **forms that match other senses in other courses** (Gaussian units and Gaussian surfaces, the adjugate "adjoint" of 18.02, classical radial equations, permutation parity, amplitude-ratio reflection and transmission coefficients in 8.03, angular-momentum raising operators, fitting degeneracies), plus missing hyphenated forms. 9 errors, 9 warnings, 11 info.
