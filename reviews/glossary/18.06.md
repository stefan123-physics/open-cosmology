# Glossary review: data/glossary/18.06.json

Scope and method: all 103 entries of `data/glossary/18.06.json` (MIT 18.06, Year II) were read against docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8 and the scoping rules in `lib/glossary.ts` (pages beat courses beat the general meaning; a word with no applicable meaning is not marked). Using the site's own loader and matcher (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor`, `getSite`), every lesson in every course was scanned with the whole glossary (so longest match across files applies), and for each 18.06 entry the first matched use on each page was listed with the meaning that would apply there (about 1,400 page uses). Every entry with more than one meaning, every course- or page-scoped meaning and every general meaning shown outside 18.06 was checked in context: in particular spectrum, dimension, diagonal, singular, antisymmetric, associative law, projection, intersection, union, elimination, permutation, stability, recursion, ellipse, ellipsoid, hyperbola, grounded, real part, basis, span, rank, independence, linear independence, unitary, orthonormal, subspace, vector space, zero vector, invertible, factorization, spectral theorem, quadratic form, positive definite, wavelet, Euler's formula, Fourier series, edge, loop, tree, group, similar, sparse, primitive and triangular matrix. The remaining 18.06-only entries were checked against their 18.06 lessons for facts (all numbers, such as the FFT count for n = 1024 and the Cooley–Tukey date, were recomputed or checked), and all entries for the plain-English rules (length, genus then difference, defining vocabulary, no TeX, no em dash, British spelling), `say`, `kind`, `origin`, `everyday` and forms. The loader reports no schema errors; every `related` id and every scoped page slug exists.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- projection: page meanings for `interference-soap-bubbles` and `gravitational-lensing`.

- rank: form "ranks" dropped.
- coefficient-matrix: page meaning for `entanglement-bell-teleportation`.
- linear-independence: form "linear dependence" dropped.
- diagonal: page meaning for `gravitational-waves-quadrupole-formula`; `rigid-body-coordinates-euler-angles` and `virial-theorem` added to the matrix pages; "zeros everywhere else".
- spectral-theorem: page meaning for `ground-state-bounds-spectral-theorem` (operators commuting with their adjoint).
- Info: principal axis theorem and spectral theorem now kind "maths"; ellipsoid reworded; formulas moved out of the shorts of similar, normal-equations, left-nullspace, positive-definite, Euler's formula; pseudoinverse and SVD wording fixed; 8.286 meaning for positive definite (of a metric); `say` for unitary and Hadamard; group scope extended; second sentences added for ohms-law, grounded, real-part, recursion, wavelet, dimension.
