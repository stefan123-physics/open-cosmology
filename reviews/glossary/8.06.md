# Glossary review: 8.06 Quantum Physics III (data/glossary/8.06.json)

Reviewer: independent glossary reviewer, 2026-11-19. Data and lessons were not edited.

## Summary

All 101 entries were checked against the writing standard (docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8) and against how the lessons use them. Every page where a form matches was found by re-running the matcher in `lib/glossary.ts`: whole-word, case-insensitive, longest form first, over the prose of all 523 pages, with the meaning chosen as `termMeaningFor` chooses it. That check covered all four entries with more than one meaning (cross-section, dipole, forbidden) or a scoped meaning (good-basis, transposition), and all 52 entries whose forms appear outside 8.06. 35 of those entries reach other courses, among them relativistic (43 pages), dipole (37), scattering (34), cross-section (29), non-relativistic (25), laser (16), ionisation (15), perturbation-theory (13), hamiltons-equations (12) and continuum (11). No form of an 8.06 entry clashes with a form in another glossary file, and all `related` ids resolve.

Overall verdict: **good, with fixes needed before merging.** The short meanings are clear, within 25 words, written as genus then difference, and free of TeX and em dashes. The scoped meanings of cross section (a geometric slice versus a scattering cross section) and of dipole (on the sky) are well chosen, and the 15 geometric-slice pages are listed completely. These need fixing:

- two factual errors in `long` texts (connection-formula, flux-quantum);
- three pages where a word gets the wrong sense: the CMB dipole on an 8.033 page, "a scattering of stars/galaxies" on three pages, and "weak isospin" on a Ferreras page;
- `introducedIn` left at the default for second senses, so a reader is pointed to the wrong lesson.

Findings: 5 errors, 10 warnings, 13 info.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- connection-formula: phase shift is π/4, an eighth of a cycle.
- flux-quantum: long rewritten around a closed surface such as a torus, as in the lesson.
- dipole: `maxwell-equations-four-potential` added to the sky meaning; introducedIn: general → 8.02 `electric-dipoles`, sky → 8.902 `cmb-anisotropies`.
- scattering: added a meaning "a loose, random spread of points" scoped to `galaxy-observations`, `particle-number-fluctuations`, `power-spectrum`.
- isospin: added a weak-isospin meaning scoped to Ferreras `dark-matter-particles`.
- continuum: added a spectrum meaning scoped to `one-random-variable`, `g-dwarf-problem-agn`, `rotation-curves`.
- magnetic-length: an area 2πℓ² holds one flux quantum.
- cross-section: geometric meaning introduced in 18.01 `related-rates`.
- forbidden: transition meaning introduced in `thermal-transition-rates-einstein-coefficients`, region meaning in `semiclassical-local-de-broglie-wavelength`; everyday note reworded. Not done: the 8.04 pages, because there the phrase "classically forbidden" is matched by the 8.04 entry `classically-forbidden`, so the bare word is not used on them.
- van-der-waals: split into the attraction (general) and a gas-law meaning scoped to the six 8.044/8.08 pages, introduced in 8.08 `van-der-waals-equation`.
- spin-statistics-theorem and symmetrization-postulate: "half-integer spin".
- airy-function: short no longer implies a unique solution; long names Bi.
- relativistic → 8.033 `four-vectors-doppler-aberration`, non-relativistic → 8.033 `force-acceleration-interstellar-travel`; short avoids "relativity".
- wilson-loop: long rewritten for loops that cannot be shrunk.
- gauge-transformation: added a quantum-field meaning scoped to `grand-unified-theories`, `higgs-field`.
- rydberg-formula: form "Rydberg constant" dropped. quantum-hall-effect, yukawa-potential (reduced Compton wavelength), born-approximation (wave vector), optical-theorem (formula moved to long), zeeman-effect (mⱼ): reworded.
- two-level-system: added a statistical-mechanics meaning for the three 8.044 pages, introduced in `entropy-thermodynamic-variable`.
- Respellings made consistent (-try-zer); added say for effective Hamiltonian and instantaneous eigenstate (LCAO already had spoken "L C A O").
- ionisation: headword changed to "ionization" (id `ionization`) to match the lessons; -ise spellings stay as forms.
- Coverage: phase shift, scattering amplitude, bound state, fine-structure constant, classically allowed, semiclassical approximation and Gaussian units are entries of the 8.04 writer; turning point is in 8.01; density of states belongs to 8.044. "adiabatic invariant" was left out on purpose: as a longer form it would take the match on 8.09 `action-angle-variables`, where 8.03's `adiabatic` has a page-scoped meaning that would then fail content:check; 8.03's adiabatic entry already explains adiabatic invariants.
