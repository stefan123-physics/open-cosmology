# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.05.json (MIT 8.05 Quantum Physics II)

Independent review, 2026-11-19. All 86 entries were checked; the one entry with more than one meaning (tensor product) and every scoped meaning (direct sum, hidden symmetry, normal, observable, projector, triplet) were checked page by page.

What was checked:

- Every form of every entry was matched against the prose of every page in `content/` with the code in `lib/glossary.ts` itself (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor`, all glossary files loaded, so longest-form competition between files is real). For each page where a form would be marked, the meaning that applies there was compared with the sentence around the first use. This covers the 8.05 lessons and every page in other courses that gets a general meaning (for example `normal` on 93 pages is correctly unmarked everywhere except `ground-state-bounds-spectral-theorem`; `observable` is correctly unmarked on the 8.033, 8.286, 8.902, 8.962 and Ferreras pages where it is an adjective).
- The lesson each meaning points to (`introducedIn`, or the default the code computes from bold text) was resolved and checked against programme order.
- Writing standard (docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8): every `short` is at most 25 words (longest 23), no TeX, no em dash, British spelling (American spellings appear only as forms); genus then difference; not circular; respellings, `kind`, `origin`; forms that could catch another sense. No form clashes with another glossary file.
- Facts in `short` and `long`.

## Findings

| triplet | error | The 8.05 meaning ("three states with total angular momentum one") is marked on three pages where "triplet" means a **triplet of operators** (three operators treated as the components of a vector): `spin-one-half-states-operators` ("The triplet Ŝ = (Ŝx, Ŝy, Ŝz) is only a bookkeeping device"), `vector-spaces-dimensionality` ("Triplets of operators"), `angular-momentum-algebra` ("A triplet of operators is called a vector under rotations"). Only `hyperfine-21cm-adding-angular-momenta` uses the spin sense. | Scope the spin meaning to `pages: ["hyperfine-21cm-adding-angular-momenta"]` (add 8.06 pages if they later use it) and add a meaning for `pages: ["spin-one-half-states-operators", "vector-spaces-dimensionality", "angular-momentum-algebra"]`: "A set of three operators, such as the x, y and z parts of spin, handled together like the three components of a vector." |

**Verdict:** not ready to merge. Three errors mark a wrong sense (triplet of operators on three 8.05 pages, the matrix characteristic polynomial on two 18.03 pages, a never-negative norm on an 8.962 page where it is negative) and must be fixed; the eight warnings (mostly `introducedIn` pointing to the wrong or a later lesson, plus the Larmor frequency unit slip) should be fixed in the same pass; the rest is optional polish.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- triplet: the spin meaning is now scoped to `hyperfine-21cm-adding-angular-momenta` only; the "triplet of operators" pages get no mark.
- characteristic-polynomial: added an 18.03 meaning (the polynomial from trying e^(rt), introduced in `second-order-constant-coefficients`); the general meaning now points to 18.06 `diagonalization-and-powers`.
- norm: added an 8.962 meaning (dot product of a vector with itself, which can be negative or zero).
- introducedIn: runge-lenz-vector → `spherical-well-oscillator-hydrogen`; bra → `eigenvalues-inner-products`; multiplet → `angular-momentum-algebra`; hyperfine-structure → `hyperfine-21cm-adding-angular-momenta`; magnetic-moment → 8.02 `magnetic-dipoles`; energy-eigenstate → 8.04 `stationary-states`; zero-point-energy → 8.04 `creation-annihilation-operators`; brachistochrone → 8.033 `brachistochrone-catenary`; inner-product → 18.06 `complex-matrices-fft`; left/right inverse → 18.06 `left-right-inverses-pseudoinverse`; functional → 8.033 `geodesics-calculus-of-variations`; norm → 8.04 `key-features-of-quantum-mechanics`; general tensor-product meaning → 8.962 `stress-energy-tensor`.
- variational-method: kept `ground-state-bounds-spectral-theorem`, because `one-d-spectrum-variational` never uses the words "variational method" (content:check requires the introducing page to use the word); that page is the introduction of `variational-principle` and `trial-wavefunction`.
- coupled-basis: kept the default (8.06 `hydrogen-fine-structure-pauli-dirac`); `spin-orbit-adding-angular-momentum` uses only "uncoupled basis" in its prose, so it cannot be the introduction.
- larmor-frequency: long now separates angular frequency γB and frequency γB/2π (43 MHz).
- left-inverse and right-inverse: shorts rewritten as suggested.
- spin-half: added a meaning scoped to `identical-particles-exchange-degeneracy` (isospin).
- spherical-bessel-function, dual-space ("in finite dimensions"), fine-structure (19,000), heisenberg-operator (symbol-free short), runge-lenz-vector ("scaled as in the lessons"), completeness-relation (plainer short): reworded.
- anti-hermitian: redundant form dropped. Respellings: Casimir KAH-zih-meer, Runge ROONG-uh.
- Not done: forms "magnetic dipole moment(s)" belong to the 8.02 entry `magnetic-dipole-moment`; "NMR" stays a case-insensitive form (no wrong match on the site).
