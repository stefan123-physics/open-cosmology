Reviewer: independent glossary reviewer, 2026-11-19. Branch `glossary`, commit 817fcda (worktree r15-a). Read: docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md (§2, §8, §12), lib/glossary.ts, all 285 entries of data/glossary/academic.json.

## Method

- All 285 entries read in full for the writing rules: short ≤ 25 words, genus then difference, not circular, no harder words, no em dash, British spelling, respelling, kind, origin/lang, forms.
- 133 entries checked against the lessons. The matcher of lib/glossary.ts (same `proseOf` stripping, same whole-word regex, longest form first, case rules) was rerun over all 523 `.mdx` files, and the matched prose was read: the first use on each page and, for the risky forms, every use. Checked: all 55 higher-risk entries in the brief, plus homogeneous, isotropic, blow-up, for-free, kills, significant, error, conventional (convention), hypothesis, iterate, numerically, mechanism, intrinsic, yield, of-order, to-first-order, cartoon, crude, fudge-factor, generic, hedging, manifestly, massage, moral, onset, abbreviation, ambiguous, analogy, arbitrary, assumption, bookkeeping, borderline, by-a-factor-of, by-definition, calibrate, carries-over, citation, comparable, converse, conversely, debate, dies-out, e-g, empirical, et-al, etc, explicit, extrapolate, formalism, hence, i-e, idealised, in-a-sense, in-practice, in-principle, infer, ingredient, insight, intuition, levels-off, likewise, messy, mit, motivate, naive, negligible, no-accident, notion, ocw, open-question, order-of-magnitude, paradox, pin-down, plausible, prerequisite, presumably, problem-set, qualitative, quantitative, read-off, recap, recitation, rigorous, rule-out, singles-out, special-case, subtle, thus, transcript, typo, valid, via, well-defined, plug-in, spell-out, stand-in, workhorse, placeholder, toolkit, in-disguise, by-eye, take-home-message.
- Every entry matches at least one lesson. Schema-level rules (≤ 25 words, no TeX, no em dash) pass. No American spellings in any meaning.

## Summary and verdict

**Verdict: not ready to merge. About 20 errors to fix first, nearly all of them wrong matches.** The writing is good: meanings are plain, mostly genus then difference, British, and the "Not the everyday meaning" notes are well chosen. Most of the 133 checked entries fit how the lessons use the word.

The problems come from the matcher. A form is matched wherever the string appears, so an academic meaning is shown for:

- **technical compounds** that no glossary file defines yet: "error function", "significant figures", "iterated integral", "intrinsic semiconductor", "perturbation theory", "homogeneous equation";
- **literal or unrelated senses**: "kill mosquito larvae", "tides would kill you", "balloon blown up", "blow up a small range" (magnify), "for free particles", "depends on the order of adding", "club P.".

The worst case is **homogeneous**. In 18.03, 8.03, 8.07 and 8.09 it means an equation with zero right-hand side, a homogeneous solution, or a function homogeneous of degree k. On about 20 pages the glossary would say "The same at every place".

Some fixes belong in this file: remove or change forms, add course-scoped meanings, set `caseSensitive`. Others need the course writers to add the longer compound entries ("error function", "perturbation theory" and so on), which then win as the longest match. Until those exist, the academic entry is what readers see.

## Missing words (suggestions)

- **assume / assumed / assuming** (about 108 pages): see the assumption row above.
- **up to a constant / up to a sign / up to a factor** (about 29 pages): "except for a constant (sign, factor) that does not matter here". A very common maths idiom that second-language readers misread.
- **in theory** (pairs with "in practice"), **loosely (speaking)** (8 pages), **unphysical** (5 pages; it could also go to a physics file).
- **significant figures**, **error function** and **perturbation theory**, so the wrong matches above stop. The last two are for the course writers.
- "canonical" (52 pages) is mostly a technical term ("canonical ensemble", "canonical transformation", "canonical momentum"), so it belongs to the 8.044/8.08/8.09 writers rather than here. "cf.", "viz.", "a posteriori", "ab initio" and "WLOG" are not used in any lesson, so they are not needed yet.
