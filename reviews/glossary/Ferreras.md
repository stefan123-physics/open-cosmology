# Glossary review: data/glossary/Ferreras.json

Date: 2026-11-19. Reviewer: independent glossary reviewer (Claude). File reviewed: `data/glossary/Ferreras.json` (113 entries, 114 meanings; only `luminosity` has two). Nothing was edited apart from this file.

## What was checked

- Read `docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md` §8 (writing standard) and `lib/glossary.ts` (schema, scope order pages > courses > general, whole-word, longest-form-first matching across all glossary files, case-insensitive unless `caseSensitive`).
- Re-implemented the matcher (all glossary files loaded, longest form first, hyphen and apostrophe as word characters) and ran it over the bodies of every `.mdx` page in `content/`, so a longer form from another file wins as it will on the site. For every one of the 113 entries I listed the courses where a form is matched and which meaning applies there, and read the context of every match outside the Ferreras course (8.01, 8.02, 8.033, 8.04, 8.044, 8.05, 8.06, 8.07, 8.08, 8.09, 8.286, 8.902, 8.962). No two entries share a form.
- Grepped the 17 Ferreras lessons for every risky form (forms that are a different thing, verb or everyday senses, acronyms) and checked the meanings against the lesson text, including the numbers (satellite counts, 40% smooth accretion, "a sixth" of baryons in stars, 100 pc resolution, the radius of influence, dwarf and ultra-faint mass limits, 1.95 K neutrino background, dex 10^0.2 = 1.58, X-ray band 0.01 to 10 nm = 0.12 to 124 keV).
- Plain-English rules: every short meaning is at most 25 words (longest: 25), no em dash, no TeX, British spelling throughout. Respellings checked by ear; all are plausible.

**Verdict:** a careful, accurate file. The physics is right almost everywhere and scopes are sensibly narrow (`convergence`, `shear`, `substructure`, `top-heavy`, `softening`, collider `luminosity`). Three forms name a *different* thing than their entry and are matched in lesson text today (tree code / particle–particle, haloscope / helioscope, and to a lesser degree axion / ALPs), one long meaning contradicts its lesson by a factor of about ten (radius of influence), and two general meanings are slightly too narrow for pages where they are used (scale radius in 8.902, pressure-supported in galaxy-clusters). The rest are small wording points. Fix the errors and warnings before merging.

## Findings

|---|---|---|---|
| tree-code | error | Forms "particle–particle", "particle-particle", "particle–mesh", "particle-mesh" are other methods, not tree codes. simulating-galaxy-formation line 70 ("This **particle–particle** (PP) method needs about N² operations") is marked with "A fast way of computing gravity … by grouping distant particles into cells", which is the opposite of what the sentence describes. | Remove the four forms. | haloscope | error | Forms "helioscope", "helioscopes" are a different instrument. detecting-dark-matter line 134 ("**Helioscopes** look instead for axions made in the Sun") gets "An axion detector: a tuned resonant cavity … in which dark matter axions from the halo would turn into faint microwaves", which is wrong for a helioscope (it points a magnet at the Sun and looks for X-rays). | Remove the two forms and add an entry `helioscope` (forms "helioscopes"), say "HEE-lee-oh-skohp": short "An axion detector: a strong magnet pointed at the Sun, in which axions made in the Sun's core would turn into X-rays." |
| radius-of-influence | error | Long meaning says the radius is "a few parsecs for a large black hole". (22 words) |

| scale-radius | warning | General meaning ("where the logarithmic slope is −2") is the NFW / Ferreras convention for halos. (It also does not hold for the Hernquist profile, slope −2.5 at its scale radius, plotted in dark-matter-halos.) | Short (general): "The radius in a profile's formula that sets its size, marking where the inner, shallower fall gives way to the steeper outer one." | pressure-supported | warning | Short says "Of a galaxy or star system: … the random motions of its stars". galaxy-clusters uses it three times for galaxy clusters ("Dynamical mass of a pressure-supported system", σ ~ 1000 km/s), whose members are galaxies. | Short: "Of a galaxy, star cluster or galaxy cluster: held up against gravity by the random motions of its members, not by orderly rotation." (23 words) |
| periodic-boundaries | warning | General meaning is matched on 8.044 free-fermi-gas, 8.06 fermis-golden-rule-constant-perturbations (twice), 8.08 ideal-fermi-gas and photon-and-phonon-gases, all about wavefunctions in a box. (23 words) |
| velocity-dispersion | info | General meaning says "stars or galaxies"; on 8.902 linear-perturbations it is the velocity dispersion of dark matter particles ("pressure (or velocity dispersion) can be ignored"). | "The spread of the random speeds of stars, galaxies or particles about their average, measured as the root mean square of the differences." |
| macho | info | Entry is `caseSensitive` and its long form is lower case, but weak-lensing-and-machos line 89 writes "Massive Astrophysical Compact Halo Objects", so the spelled-out name is never marked. | Add forms "Massive Astrophysical Compact Halo Object(s)". Consider `say` "MAH-choh". |
| microlensing | info | "Lensing by a star-sized object": it is the mass, not the size, that matters (a black hole is tiny). | "Lensing by an object of about a star's mass, whose images are too close to separate, seen as a background star brightening and fading." |

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed; most info items taken.

- tree-code: particle–particle and particle–mesh forms removed; new entries `particle-particle-method` and `particle-mesh-method`.
- haloscope: helioscope forms removed; new entry `helioscope`.
- radius-of-influence: long corrected to about 50 pc for 10⁷ solar masses at 30 km/s; short widened to any body.
- axion: ALP forms moved to a new entry `axion-like-particle`; the axion long no longer mentions ALPs.
- nambu-goldstone-boson, pressure-supported, periodic-boundaries, scale-radius (short general, NFW slope in the long): shorts rewritten as proposed.
- hot-mode-accretion: cold-mode forms dropped; cold mode described in the long.
- Info: velocity-dispersion, microlensing, strong-lensing, bondi-accretion, low-surface-brightness-galaxy, cosmic-neutrino-background, einasto-profile, effective-radius, aqual, luminosity (collider long) reworded or extended; MACHO gets the capitalised long forms and a respelling; scintillator forms dropped; wimpless-miracle kind set to physics; one-sentence longs extended. n-body-simulation left here (harmless); no introducedIn added, since the default (first bold) points to the right Ferreras lessons; in quadrature, softening and sphere of influence left as they are (no wrong use today).
