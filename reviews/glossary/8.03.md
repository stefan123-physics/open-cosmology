# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.03.json (MIT 8.03 Vibrations and Waves)

Independent review, 2026-11-19. All 82 entries were checked; the entries with more than one meaning (adiabatic, carrier, kink, resolution, separation of variables) were checked page by page.

What was checked:

- Every form of every entry was matched against the prose of every lesson in `content/` with the same rules as `lib/glossary.ts` (whole words, case-insensitive unless `caseSensitive`, longest form first, first use per page, scope pages > courses > general). For each page that would be marked, the meaning that applies there was compared with the sentence around the first use. This covers the 8.03 lessons and every page in other courses where a general meaning would be shown.
- Scoped `pages` exist and really use the word in that sense; `introducedIn` pages exist and explain the idea.
- Writing standard (docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §8): `short` at most 25 words (all pass; the longest is 25), genus then difference, not circular, no harder word than the headword, no TeX, no em dash (none found), British spelling; respellings in `say`, `kind`, `everyday`, `origin`, and forms that could catch a different sense.
- Facts (numbers and claims in `short` and `long`).

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed; most info items taken.

- **dispersion**: added a velocity-dispersion meaning (courses 8.902, Ferreras) and a "short for the dispersion relation" meaning for the three 8.08 pages.
- **linearity**: the general meaning is now the broad "sum in, sum out" property of a rule; the solutions wording is scoped to 8.03 and 18.03; a "straight-line relation" meaning covers `anthropic-multiverse` and `cosmological-principle-expansion`.
- **separation-of-variables**: 8.03 and 8.07 now have separate meanings (8.07 introduced in `separation-of-variables-cartesian`, with a `long` on Laplace's equation); `hamilton-jacobi-equation` has its own meaning with a sum, not a product.
- **steady-state**: meaning for the steady-state theory, scoped to `homogeneous-expansion`.
- **kink**: `introducedIn` is now `generating-em-waves`.
- **uncertainty-principle**: short broadened to pairs of quantities, `long` mentions fields; kind changed to physics.
- **probability-wave**: "whose size, squared, …".
- **electric-displacement**: now "chosen so that Gauss's law counts only free charges".
- **wave-number**: spectroscopy meaning (1/λ) for `born-oppenheimer-approximation`.
- **surface-mass-density**: lensing meaning scoped to Ferreras.
- **carrier**: general short no longer circular.
- Info items taken: envelope (row of peaks), stiffness (general `long`), delta-function (18.03 sentence moved to a course-scoped meaning with a `here`), the three polarization genera ("The state of light in which…"), total internal reflection, grazing incidence, quark-gluon plasma ("first few microseconds"), small-angle approximation (1 per cent at 14 degrees), Chladni respelling "KLAHT-nee". Not taken: phased array rename, Cramer respelling, -ise/-ize mix (both British).
