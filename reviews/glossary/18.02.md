# Glossary review: 18.02 (Multivariable Calculus)

Reviewed 2026-11-19, independently of the writer, against `docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md` §2 and §8 and the matching rules in `lib/glossary.ts`. Matching is whole-word, and hyphens count as word characters, so "inverse-square" and "curl-free" do not match. Scoped meanings win: pages, then courses, then the general meaning.

**What was checked:** all 70 entries in `data/glossary/18.02.json`. For each one I checked:

- the meaning against the 18.02 lesson that introduces the word (Jacobian ∂(u, v)/∂(x, y) with du dv = |J| dx dy; second-derivative test with A = f_xx, B = f_xy, C = f_yy and AC − B²; spherical ρ, φ measured from the z-axis, θ);
- every page in other courses where a form would be marked (using the usage helper, then grep, reading the sentence that would get the first mark);
- the plain-English rules (word counts, em dashes, TeX, British spelling);
- the respellings and kinds;
- `related`, `symbols`, `pages` and `introducedIn` against the existing ids and slugs.

All `short` meanings are 25 words or fewer. There are no em dashes and no TeX, the spelling is British, and every `related`, symbol, `pages` and `introducedIn` id exists (`velocity` → 8.01 position-and-velocity-1d, `moment-of-inertia` → 8.01 moment-of-inertia, `lorentz-transformation` → 8.033 lorentz-transformation are all sensible). The respellings are reasonable.

**Verdict:** the maths is sound, and most entries match the 18.02 lessons. The main problem is scope. Several general meanings are written only for the 18.02 sense, and they would put a wrong meaning on pages in other courses. The cases are: change of variables, Jacobian, divergence, velocity field, cusp, triple product, adjoint matrix, minor, level surface and curl (as a verb). The spherical-coordinates short is also inaccurate. Fix the errors before merging.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

All errors and warnings fixed, plus most info items.

1. **curl:** dropped the form "curls". 8.07 is no longer in the course scope; the curl meaning is scoped page by page to the six 8.07 pages that use the operator, so `vector-analysis` ("Curl the fingers") is left out. 8.01 and 8.02 use only the verb and stay unscoped.
2. **triple-product:** new entry `triple-product-rule`; the longer form wins on 8.044 `thermodynamic-variables`.
3. **adjoint-matrix:** scoped to 18.02 and 18.06.
4. **jacobian:** added a Jacobian-matrix meaning for 18.03 and 8.09.
5. **change-of-variables:** the short is now general ("Rewriting a problem in new variables suited to it…"). The long fixes the direction: du dv = |J| dx dy with J = ∂(u, v)/∂(x, y), and dx dy = r dr dθ for polar coordinates. It also mentions differential equations and mechanics.
6. **divergence:** added a meaning for "growing without limit", scoped to `shooting-method`, `bose-einstein-condensation`, `thermal-radiation`, `black-body-radiation`, `vacuum-energy-puzzle` and `ginzburg-landau-theory`. Also added an 18.01 meaning for series.
7. **level-curve:** removed the forms "level surface(s)".
8. **minor:** now scoped to 18.02, plus the 18.06 page `determinant-formulas-cofactors`, so "minor axis" is no longer marked. No "minor axis" entry was added, because neither of us owns it; that is noted for the lead.
9. **spherical-coordinates:** the short now uses no globe analogy. The long notes that physics books swap θ and φ.
10. **local-maximum:** `related` now lists local-minimum instead of itself.
- **Warnings:** added an 18.03 meaning for velocity-field (the arrows of a system of ODEs). Simply connected is now defined by loops shrinking to a point, and the long covers space minus a point. Cusp got a meaning for the two 8.08 pages (a corner where the slope jumps). The Laplacian and diffusion-equation shorts no longer contain a formula or the word Laplacian. The iterated-integral wording now refers to the outermost integral.
- **Info:** the divergence theorem is now kind `maths`. "Where f is positive" added to double-integral. Form "parameterisation" added. Left as they are: the kind of degenerate (physics, with an 18.02 meaning), the 8.286 saddle-shaped surface (the general meaning fits), the one-variable second-derivative test (the general short fits), and notation in a few longs.
