# Glossary review: data/glossary/8.09.json

Scope and method: all 174 entries of `data/glossary/8.09.json` (MIT 8.09, Year IV) were read against docs/GLOSSARY-PLAN.md §2 and §8 and the scoping rules in `lib/glossary.ts` (page beats course beats general). The check used the site's own loader and matcher (`loadGlossary`, `buildMatcher`, `proseOf`, `termMeaningFor`) over every lesson in every course. For each 8.09 entry it listed the first matched use on each page (longest match first, across all glossary files) and the meaning that would apply there. Every multi-meaning or scoped entry, and every general meaning that reaches a page outside 8.09, was read in context. The 8.09 uses were checked against the lessons for a large sample of entries (well over 70; list at the end). Facts, numbers and dates in the longs were checked, along with `say`, `kind`, `origin`, `everyday`, the plain-English rules (short ≤ 25 words, no TeX, no em dash) and spelling. The loader reports no schema errors for this file. Every `related` and `symbols` id resolves, no short is over 25 words, and there are no em dashes or TeX. "-ize" spellings (generalized, linearization) follow the site's existing usage and the other glossary files.

Overall, the definitions are accurate, clear and well matched to Stewart's notes. The problems are mostly about scope: a few general meanings reach pages where the word means something else ("fixed point", "total derivative", "normal coordinates", "Euler equation"). There is also one convention mismatch: the action variable is defined with a 2π that the lessons do not use.

## Verdict

Not ready to merge. Fix the 6 errors and 7 warnings first. Most need a page-scoped meaning, a narrower scope, or a longer entry so that longest match takes the phrase. The info items are optional.

## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- fixed-point: the dynamics meaning is now scoped to the six nonlinear-dynamics pages, with a `here` note; no general meaning, so "a fixed point" in the sense of a pivot or fixed place is no longer marked.
- euler-equation: `symmetric-top-one-point-fixed` added to the rigid-body meaning.
- action-variable, action-angle-variables, angle-variable: rewritten to the notes' convention J = ∮ p dq (angle advances by 1 per cycle), with the 2π convention mentioned as the alternative.
- total-derivative: general short reworded; new page-scoped meaning "exactly the derivative of one function" for the five pages listed plus `canonical-transformations-generating-functions`; forms "total time derivative(s)" added.
- normal-coordinates: handled by a "Riemann normal coordinates" entry in data/glossary/8.962.json (longest match takes the phrase).
- lagrangian: fluid meaning scoped to `simulating-galaxy-formation` only; `here` note removed.
- action: page meaning for "laser action" on `one-dimensional-bifurcations`.
- map: page meaning "a transformation" for `hamiltonian-generators-conservation-laws` and `symmetry-conservation-laws`.
- chaos: page meaning for "chaotic initial conditions" on `false-vacuum`.
- isothermal: new entry "isothermal sphere" (with "singular isothermal sphere").
- stokes-theorem: short now says "the flux of a vector field's curl through a surface".
- Info items: `universal` scope widened with a broader long; "streamline flow" form on `laminar`; 2D note on `point-source`; generating-function and Euler's theorem shorts reworded; respellings for Jacobi, Lyapunov, separatrix changed as suggested; duplicate d'Alembert form dropped; `dissipative` short reads for the noun too; everyday note on `bottleneck`; Lyapunov entries now kind "named". The site-wide kind rule for other eponyms (Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, Eulerian) is left to the lead.
