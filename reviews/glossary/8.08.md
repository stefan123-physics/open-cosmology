## Resolution (2026-11-19, by the writer)

- **heat-conductivity:** the long now says "a sixth".
- **mobility:** the long now gives the Einstein relation instead of the conductivity formula.
- **energy-band:** degenerate-fermi-gas was removed from the crystal-band scope and given its own meaning (the spin bands shifted by a field).
- **time-of-flight:** there is now a general meaning (the time a projectile is in the air), and the cold-atom method is scoped to 8.08.
- **vacuum:** the short now reads "a state with no particles, usually the one of lowest energy", and the long mentions false vacua and the landscape. The everyday note was reworded. A "false vacuum" entry exists in the 8.01 file, which other writers are working on, so it will take that phrase once merged.
- **reversible:** the long now defines a reversible change in general (dS = dQ/T) and gives Wen's adiabatic spin example separately.
- **statistics:** the longs are now general, and the particle meaning is introduced in 8.06 identical-particles.
- **cut-off, saha-equation (short, deuterium, SHAH-hah), phase-transition, bose-distribution and fermi-dirac-distribution (formula with brackets), pauli-susceptibility (no underscore, one spin state), brillouin-zone (electrons or vibrations, bree-YWAN), potential-barrier ("or a system"), ginzburg-landau-theory (GINZ-burg):** reworded as suggested.
- **One-sentence longs:** a second sentence was added to all the listed entries.
- **Info items:** done for brownian-motion, vortex (cause and effect), drift (+ dc-circuits), additive (+ grand-canonical-ensemble, a long, kind physics), correlation (covariance note), excluded-volume (four times the volume) and effective-field (long). Kept as they are: the -ize (Oxford) spellings, which the site's other glossary files also use; the exponent notation with ^; and the power-spectrum meanings.
