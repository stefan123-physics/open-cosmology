## Verdict

Not ready to merge. The meanings themselves are mostly accurate, clearly written and correctly numbered, and the course scopes on implicit, explicit, composition and limits of integration work well. But a few very common words put a wrong meaning on many pages: "limit" (about 50 pages: "without limit", bounds, limit cycles, the central limit theorem), "continuous" (14 pages with the continuity equation), "differential" (18 pages of adjective uses), plus smaller clusters for diverge, zeros, converge and "normal curve". Most of these are fixed by adding a few phrase entries ("without limit", "limit cycle", "central limit theorem", "continuity equation", "differential cross section", "differential operator" …), which win because the longest form matches first, and a handful of page-scoped meanings. Fix the two factual errors (transcendental, rational-function) and the spelling, then rerun the first-use dump for limit, continuous and differential.

Counts: 13 errors, 14 warnings, 10 info.
