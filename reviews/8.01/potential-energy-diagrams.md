- **Stable versus unstable.** The transcript does not name the coordinates, but its slope arguments settle them. 25.2 p. 3: at the first point, "over here the slope is negative … F of x is positive … it moves off to the right", and on the other side "the slope is positive, but the force is negative". The particle moves away on both sides, so the transcript calls it unstable. For $U = 4x^2 - 2x^3$ that pattern (slope positive on the left, negative on the right) occurs only at the maximum, $x = 4/3$ m. At the second point the force is restoring on both sides, so it is stable: the minimum at $x = 0$ (pp. 3–4). The page agrees, and so does the notes' Example 14.1.
